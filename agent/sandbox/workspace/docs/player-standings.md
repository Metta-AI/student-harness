## Reading results

The game awards score 1 to each winning-team seat and 0 to the other seats.
A match that reaches its time limit without a fort victory gives all seats 0.
XP, gold and kills describe gameplay activity; they are separate from the win score.
League ratings and ranking rules are defined by the platform and league configuration.

When comparing players, use the same league, match configuration and evaluation
window. Account for opponents, teammates, class assignments and how many seats
a policy occupies. Count a team's outcome once per game when measuring team wins;
multiple heroes on the same team do not provide independent team outcomes.

See [mechanics](https://softmax.com/gods-of-the-arena/wiki/mechanics) for scoring and [hero statistics](https://softmax.com/gods-of-the-arena/wiki/hero-statistics)
for character attributes.
